Screen Off, Welcome To the Real World

By: JinSuper | 金旭
Class: 11 / Grade 8

It's no secret that our digital life has made everything much easier. Artificial Intelligence (AI) and robots can do dangerous jobs for us. Tablets, smartphones, and other mobile devices help us get the latest messages and communicate with others quickly. What's more, internet memes create unprecedented interest among people.

However, excessive use of smartphones and addiction to the virtual world might expose us to too much fragmented and junk information. They make us numb, rob us of the joy of doing everything, and destroy our ability to focus. Some people overuse internet memes in real life; this might seriously affect others who are confused or feel lost.[1]

So, it's time for us to take action against digital addiction! Victory is within your reach as long as you make an effort and believe in yourself! Turn off your screen. Next, embrace the wonderful, real world![2]

数字化浪潮让生活变得便捷，这早已不是秘密。人工智能与机器人为我们承担了危险的工作。平板、手机等移动设备，助我们迅速获取讯息，与人交流。更有甚者，网络迷因在人们中间引发了前所未有的兴致。

然而，过度使用手机与沉迷虚拟世界，可能让我们饱受碎片化与垃圾信息的侵扰。它们令我们麻木，剥夺了做事的乐趣，摧毁了专注的能力。有些人甚至在现实生活里滥用网络迷因，这或许会深深影响那些困惑、迷茫的人。

所以，是时候对数字成瘾宣战了！只要肯付出努力并相信自己，胜利便触手可及！关上屏幕，去拥抱美好而真实的现实世界吧！
